Plan your renovation with Claude

Remont is a free MCP connector that turns a chat with Claude into a complete renovation plan: work order, materials calculated from your room sizes, prices from local DIY stores, contractor rates, a checklist, a floor layout and a 3D view.

How it works

  1. Add the connector to Claude (once).
  2. Tell Claude about your home or send a floor plan photo — or fill in the short questionnaire.
  3. Get an interactive plan: change materials, switch DIY / contractor, tick tasks, save it.

Connect

Claude (web, desktop, mobile)

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Connectors (in newer versions: Customize → Connectors).
  3. Click “Add custom connector” (or “+”).
  4. Name: Remont. URL: the server URL above. No OAuth or keys needed — click Add.
  5. In a chat, open the “+” / tools menu and make sure Remont is on. Then just ask about your renovation.

Free plan: one custom connector. Team / Enterprise: an owner adds it in the organisation settings first. Once added, it also works in the Claude desktop and mobile apps.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The connector is free and needs no sign-in. You need an account in Claude (or another MCP client); whether you can add custom connectors depends on your plan there.

Where do the prices come from?

Claude looks them up in the DIY stores of your country and links every product. Where no store price is found yet, the plan shows an approximate price marked ≈.

What about my data?

No account and no personal data. Plans are stored under a random code; plan links are unguessable and not indexed.

Which countries?

22 countries are built in, including Poland, Germany, the UK, the US, Canada and Ukraine. For other countries Claude adds the currency and stores itself.
